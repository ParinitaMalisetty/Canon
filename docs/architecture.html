Using these in slides. Each plate is a single inline SVG on a white ground — screenshot the plate area at 2× and it drops into a slide at full resolution with no rasterization artefacts. Figures 1 and 2 are the architecture pair; 3 and 4 are the supporting figures a methodology section usually needs alongside them.

Why grayscale. Same constraint as the console: every distinction here is carried by weight, fill density, dash pattern or position rather than hue, so the figures survive a black-and-white print and a projector with the contrast turned down.
